FAQ

Questions, answered

Does LicenseFlow tell me my state's exact requirements?

LicenseFlow models your licensing path and guides you through it. This build shows sample requirements for demonstration — always confirm exact hours, fees, and steps with your state Department of Insurance or NIPR. Verified state data can be loaded into the platform's rules layer.

Is this an online course?

No. It's a licensing operating system. Education is one stage — the platform also guides exam prep, fingerprints, application, and state review from start to license.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Every account is protected by per-user database security, so only you can see your own progress, documents, and results.
