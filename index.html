FAQ

Questions, answered

Does LicenseFlow work for my state?

Yes — LicenseFlow supports agents in all 50 states. You set your state during signup, and we tailor CE requirements and reminders accordingly. Always confirm exact figures with your state Department of Insurance.

Is this official exam prep or state-approved CE?

LicenseFlow is a study and organization tool. It helps you learn the material and stay on top of your credits and deadlines, but it does not itself issue state-approved CE credit. Always complete CE through your state-approved providers and log it here.

Is my information secure?

Absolutely. Every agent account is protected by per-user security rules, so only you can view your own credits, study progress, and support tickets.

How much does it cost?

You can start completely free with the Starter plan. Pro adds priority support and reminders for $19/month, and Agency plans are available for teams.
